Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the size of the space, the finishes you choose, and the condition of what is already there. We walk the property first, then give you a written scope and price before anything starts.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. The consultation, the on-site walkthrough, and the written estimate are free.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Yes — licensed with the California Contractors State License Board under #000000, bonded, and carrying general liability and workers' compensation coverage.

Do you handle plans and permits?

We do. We coordinate the drawings, pull the permits, and schedule the inspections so you are not sitting at the city counter on your day off.

How long will my project take?

A bathroom is usually a few weeks; an addition or ADU runs a few months once permits are issued. You get a start date and a finish date in writing with the estimate.

What areas do you serve?

Long Beach and the surrounding South Bay and Southeast Los Angeles County cities, including Signal Hill, Lakewood, Carson, Torrance, and Bellflower.
